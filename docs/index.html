Condensed Matter Research Log

A student research project in superconductivity and computational materials physics, aimed at the 2027 science fair season. Last updated: 2026-11-14.

Overall progress

Current focus

Flagship question: do machine-learning predictors of superconductor critical temperature (Tc) still work on chemical families they have never seen, and do crystal-structure features help?

Honest status: leave-one-cluster-out validation for Tc was already introduced by Meredig et al. (2018). This project is a replication and extension on the newer 3DSC dataset, not a claim of a brand-new idea. A literature check in Stage 3.1 decides whether the angle stays or switches to a backup.
